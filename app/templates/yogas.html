YOGA PHILOSOPHY

In Vedic Astrology, a Yoga is a specific mathematical and spatial relationship between planets. While some indicate wealth and power (Raja Yoga), others highlight spiritual depth (Sanyasa Yoga) or karmic tests (Dur Yoga). The true strength of a Yoga is determined by the **Dignity** and **Shadbala** of its participating planets.
