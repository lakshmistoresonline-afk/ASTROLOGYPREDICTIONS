## 3. OBSERVATIONS
- **High Sensitivity**: The model successfully detects 80% of major breakthroughs.
- **Low Specificity**: The model incorrectly "detects" breakthroughs in 80% of stable periods.
- **The "Always-On" Problem**: For high-capacity charts (Jobs, Diana, Einstein), the system reports strong success almost continuously during their peak dasha years.

## 4. NEXT STEPS
Hardening the **Trigger Window** is required to reduce the False Positive Rate (FPR) from 80% to <30%.
