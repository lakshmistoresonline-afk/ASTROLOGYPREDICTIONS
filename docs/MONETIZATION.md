# Astro Predictions — Monetization Strategy

## 1. Freemium Model
- **Free Plan**: Basic birth chart, Moon sign, Nakshatra, basic Dasha overview, limited daily insights, and ad-supported experience.
- **Astro Plus Subscription (Monthly / Yearly)**: Full prediction domains, advanced Dasha & transits, full Life Timeline intelligence, and an ad-free experience.

## 2. One-Time Purchases (Premium Reports)
- Career Report, Marriage Report, Finance Report, Business Report, Property Report, Annual Report, Life Timeline Report, Complete Vedic Report.

## 3. Advertising
- Non-intrusive ad placement for free-tier users, disabled for Astro Plus subscribers via `EntitlementService` and `AdService`.
