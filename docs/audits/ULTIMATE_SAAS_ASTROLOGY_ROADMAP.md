## 1. Advanced Visualizations & Interactive Intelligence Dashboards

### A. Shadbala & Planetary Potency Breakdown Charts
- **Visual Breakdown**: Interactive radar or bar charts displaying Sthana Bala, Dig Bala, Kala Bala, Chesta Bala, Naisargika Bala, and Drik Bala scores for every planet in the chart.
- **Why it matters**: Gives users instant clarity on whether a planet is debilitated or exalted in actual numerical potency.

### B. Ashtakavarga Transit Simulator
- **Interactive Tool**: Visual Sarvashtakavarga (SAV) and Bhinnashtakavarga (BAV) bindu heatmaps across the 12 houses to show auspicious transit windows.
- **Why it matters**: Allows users to check exactly which days bring financial or career gains based on transit bindu counts.

### C. Advanced Synastry & Relationship Deep-Dive
- **Comprehensive Matching**: Expanding beyond basic Guna Milan to include Manglik dosha cancellation analysis, 7th house lord synergy, and Navamsha overlay comparisons.
- **Why it matters**: Essential for marriage compatibility and long-term partnership forecasting.
