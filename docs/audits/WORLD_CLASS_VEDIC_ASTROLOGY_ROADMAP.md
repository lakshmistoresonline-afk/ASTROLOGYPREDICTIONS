- **Shadbala (Sixfold Potency)**: Implement rigorous mathematical calculations for the six sources of planetary strength (Sthana Bala, Dig Bala, Kala Bala, Chesta Bala, Naisargika Bala, Drik Bala) to numerically rank planet potency in the chart.

## 2. Comprehensive Shodashvarga (16 Divisional Charts)

## 3. Micro-Timing & Multi-Dasha Systems
- **Deep Vimshottari Sub-Cycles**: Compute Vimshottari Dasha down to Pratyantar (sub-sub) and Sookshma (sub-sub-sub) levels for exact day-by-day life timing.
- **Alternative Dasha Systems**: Support Yogini Dasha, Ashtottari Dasha, and Jaimini Chara Dasha for robust cross-system predictive corroboration.

## 4. Personalized Vedic Remedial (Upaya) Intelligence
- **Gemstone Prescription**: Precise gemstone recommendations based on functional benefics and Yogakaraka planets, with weight, metal, finger, and mantra protocols, while actively warning against functional malefics.
- **Mantra & Charity Sadhana**: Customized remedial prescriptions including specific planetary beeja mantras, Vedic charity days, and fasting recommendations keyed to active weak or afflicted planets.

## 5. Interactive SVG Chart & Visual Life Timeline
- **Interactive Kundli Renderers**: Dynamic SVG North Indian and South Indian chart wheels with interactive house inspection, aspect (Drishti) ray overlays, and planet degree popups.
- **Graphical Life Atlas**: Visual time-series graph displaying composite astrological confidence curves across career, finance, marriage, and health over decades.
