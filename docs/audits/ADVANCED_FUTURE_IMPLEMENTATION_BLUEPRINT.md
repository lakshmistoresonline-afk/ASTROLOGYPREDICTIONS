# Astro Predictions — Advanced Future Implementation Blueprint

To make Astro Predictions the undisputed best and most detailed Vedic astrology prediction application in existence, this engineering blueprint defines advanced predictive modules, deep divisional intelligence, quantitative strength algorithms, and interactive client-side visualizers.

---

## 1. Advanced Predictive Modules & Implementation Roadmap

### A. Comprehensive Shadbala (Sixfold Planetary Strength) Engine
- **Mathematical Computation**: Implement exact calculations for Sthana Bala (positional strength), Dig Bala (directional strength), Kala Bala (temporal strength), Chesta Bala (motional strength), Naisargika Bala (natural strength), and Drik Bala (aspectual strength).
- **Practical Application**: Use numerical Shadbala totals to determine whether a planet is fully capable of delivering its natal promise or whether remedial strengthening (Ratna/Mantra) is required.

### B. Shodashvarga (16 Divisional Charts) Deep Synthesis
- **Divisional Integration**: Fully compute all 16 classical vargas (D1 through D60) with Vargottama placement flags, planetary dignity across vargas (Simhasanaamsa, Parijatamsa, etc.), and divisional house lord connections.

### C. Micro-Timing (Pratyantar & Sookshma Dasha)
- **Granular Timing**: Expand Vimshottari Dasha calculations down to Pratyantar (sub-sub-period) and Sookshma (sub-sub-sub-period) to pinpoint exact dates of major life events.

### D. Interactive SVG Kundli & Graphical Life Atlas UI
- **Interactive Charts**: Build interactive North Indian and South Indian SVG chart renderers where users can hover over houses to see occupants, degrees, aspects, and lordships.
- **Visual Life Timeline**: Interactive time-series charts showing planetary transit peaks and dasha life phase transitions over decades.
