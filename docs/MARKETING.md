# Astro Predictions — Marketing Strategy & Positioning

## 1. Core Positioning
Astro Predictions is positioned as **Vedic Astrology Intelligence**, moving beyond generic daily horoscopes to deliver precision chart-based insights, Vimshottari Dasha life timing, and planetary transits calculated via the Swiss Ephemeris.

## 2. Target Audience
- Seekers of authentic Vedic astrology (Jyotish)
- Professionals looking for career and financial timing guidance
- Individuals seeking relationship compatibility and life milestone forecasting

## 3. Acquisition Channels
- **SEO & Content Hub (`/learn`, `/vedic-astrology`, `/dasha`, `/transits`)**: Organic search acquisition targeting Vedic astrology terminology.
- **Free Birth Chart Funnel (`/birth-chart`)**: Low-friction lead generation providing immediate value (Lagna, Moon sign, Nakshatra, Dasha overview).
- **Social Sharing & Referral Loops (`/share/<token>`, `/r/<code>`)**: Viral growth via shareable astrological signal cards and referral codes.
- **Android App Store & PWA**: Mobile distribution via Google Play and Progressive Web App installation.
