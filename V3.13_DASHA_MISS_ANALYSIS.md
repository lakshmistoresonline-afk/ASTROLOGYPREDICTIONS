# V3.13 Dasha Miss Analysis

## 1. NODE PROXY MISSES
A significant number of missed events occur during the Antardasha of **Rahu** or **Ketu**.
- **Case**: Michael Jordan (Career Breakthrough). Dasha: Ketu/Rahu.
- **Problem**: Rahu acts as the lord of the sign it occupies. In Jordan's chart, Rahu was acting as Mercury (11th Lord).
- **Result**: The current engine treats Rahu as "Neutral" (Score 60) unless specifically listed as a lord. This results in a "Stability Phase" prediction instead of "Activation."

## 2. TRANSITION PERIODS
Events occurring during Dasha transition windows (within 30 days of a new Antar) are often missed if the previous Antar lord is not relevant but the new one is.
- **Finding**: Events often manifest *before* the supportive Antar starts, as the "Build Up" phase.

## 3. RECOMMENDATION
Update all domain engines to resolve **Rahu/Ketu sign-lord proxies** during the DASHA_ACTIVATION audit.
