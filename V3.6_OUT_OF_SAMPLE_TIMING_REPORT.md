## 2. OBSERVATIONS

## 3. ERROR ANALYSIS
The median timing error was not calculable for high precision because the engine's current "Proximity Weighting" (V3.5) was often too conservative for the historical holdout dates, favoring the "Background Potential" phase over a sharp "Peak Manifestation."
