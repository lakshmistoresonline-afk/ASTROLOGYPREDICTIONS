# UI Dashboard Guide

The ASTROLOGYPREDICTIONS dashboard is designed to present complex Vedic data in an actionable, tiered format.

## 🧭 Dashboard Navigation

### 1. Resonance Center (Command Center)
- **Score Portal**: Displays your current "Cosmic Resonance" (Overall support score).
- **Limb Snapshot**: Quick view of today's Vara, Tithi, and Yoga.
- **AI Insight**: Optional mystical summary from a local LLM (Ollama).

### 2. Temporal Map (Life progression)
A horizontal scrolling timeline showing your **Vimshottari Dasha** cycles.
- **Active Cycle**: Your current Mahadasha and its core theme.
- **House Activation**: Indicates which natal houses are currently prominent.

### 3. Monthly Synthesis
A strategic overview of the current month based on:
- **Transit Alignment**: Major planetary movements.
- **Opportunities/Cautions**: Specific life areas highlighted for growth or patience.

### 4. Bhava Intelligence (House-by-House)
An accordion-style breakdown of all 12 cosmic departments:
- **Theme**: Primary life area (e.g., Wealth, Career).
- **Power Zone**: Functional strength based on Lord dignity and SAV points.
- **Interpretation**: Detailed logic derived from the deterministic engine.

### 5. Prediction Vectors
The core insights grouped into logical sections:
- **Essence**: Identity and Life Purpose.
- **Material**: Career, Business, and Wealth.
- **Social**: Family and Relationships.
- **Survival**: Health, Legal, and Longevity.

Every card contains **Cosmic Evidence** (The "Why") and **Timing Peak** (The "When").
