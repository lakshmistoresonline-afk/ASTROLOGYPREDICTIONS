# V3.4 Selection Bias & Dataset Audit

## 1. DATASET COMPOSITION
- **Total Cases**: 10
- **Positive Cases (Events)**: 5 (Steve Jobs IPO, Diana Wedding, Einstein Papers, Gates IPO, Musk SpaceX)
- **Negative Cases (Stability)**: 5 (Matched periods of stability for the same individuals)

## 2. SELECTION METHODOLOGY
- **Positive Selection**: Hand-picked major global breakthroughs where birth data and event dates are verified (A-grade data).
- **Negative Selection**: Manual selection of documented "quiet" periods (e.g., student years, childhood) in the same life-domains as the positive events.

## 3. BIAS AUDIT
- **Outcome Bias**: High. The positive cases are extreme outliers (world-changing breakthroughs).
- **Temporal Bias**: The dataset is skewed towards 20th-century events.
- **Geographic Bias**: Dominated by US and European charts.
- **Algorithmic Bias**: Detected. The current engine is optimized to "find the promise," which leads to high scores in any period where the Natal and Dasha foundations are strong.

## 4. SAMPLE SIZE LIMITATIONS
> [!IMPORTANT]
> EARLY SIGNAL (N=10): The current dataset is sufficient to identify structural flaws (False Positives) but is not yet a statistically significant measure of real-world accuracy.
