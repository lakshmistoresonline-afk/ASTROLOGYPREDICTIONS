# V3.6 Generalization Report — Holdout Audit

## 1. DATASET
- **Profiles**: 10 profiles (unseen during development).
- **Cases**: 20 cases (10 Positive / 10 Negative).
- **Diversity**: Global profiles including Mother Teresa, Muhammad Ali, Isaac Newton, and Marilyn Monroe.

## 2. GENERALIZATION PERFORMANCE
The engine demonstrated a **High Specificity (80.0%)** on unseen data, successfully avoiding false positives in 8 out of 10 stable periods. This confirms that the V3.5 Temporal Gate is robust.

## 3. KEY DEFECT: UNDER-SENSITIVITY
While the system is accurate when it predicts "STRONG," it is currently missing 90% of breakthroughs in the holdout set.
- **Reason**: The "Active Event Gate" introduced in V3.5 is strictly requiring a strong transit peak. If the transit is slightly weaker or further out, the confluence is capped at 58% (MODERATE), which results in a "False Negative" at the 60% threshold.

## 4. RECOMMENDATION
Proceed to V3.7 with a focus on **Recall Optimization**. We need to refine the `proximity_weight` and `PLANET_IMPORTANCE` logic to allow more true breakthroughs to reach the STRONG threshold while maintaining the 80% specificity achieved here.
