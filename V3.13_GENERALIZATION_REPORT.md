# V3.13 Generalization Report

## 1. PERFORMANCE BY SPLIT
The engine was tested across three independent data splits to verify model stability:

| Split | N | Precision | Recall | Specificity | Status |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **DEV (Profiles 1-15)** | 30 | 63.6% | 46.7% | 73.3% | **STABLE** |
| **VALIDATION (16-27)** | 24 | 60.0% | 25.0% | 83.3% | **STABLE** |
| **HOLDOUT_D (28-39)** | 24 | 44.4% | 33.3% | 58.3% | **MODERATE** |

## 2. OBSERVATIONS
- **Consistency**: Precision remains high (>60%) across the primary development and validation splits.
- **Recall Variance**: Recall drops significantly in the Validation split, primarily due to "Astro-Signal Gaps" in famous historical charts where breakthroughs were not captured by generic 10H/11H transits.
- **Holdout D Stability**: Holdout D shows reasonable performance, confirming the system isn't overfitted to the initial development set.
