# Remedy (Upaya) Engine Documentation

The engine provides traditional Vedic remedies for planetary afflictions and specific house-based challenges.

## ⚙️ Logic Pipeline
The engine analyzes the chart for:
1. **Debilitation**: Planets in their weakest signs.
2. **Combustion**: Planets too close to the Sun.
3. **Dusthana Influence**: Functional malefics in houses 6, 8, or 12.
4. **Difficult Yogas**: Presence of combinations like Kemadruma Yoga.

## 🛠️ Types of Remedies

### 1. Mantras (Sound Vibration)
Standard beeja mantras for each planet to harmonize its energy.

### 2. Charity (Dana)
Specific donations recommended on the planet's day (e.g., Sunday for Sun).

### 3. Lifestyle (Aachara)
Practical behavioral changes to align with the planet's higher principles.

### 4. Lifestyle Quests (Gamified Upayas)
A unique "Quest" system that transforms traditional remedies into actionable growth goals. Example:
- **The Stoic's Endurance (Saturn)**: Volunteering and community service.
- **The Artist's Grace (Venus)**: Engaging in creative hobbies.

## 💎 Gemstone Guidance
Provides traditional information on gemstones, suitable fingers, and metals for each planet. 
**Caution**: Users are always advised to consult a practitioner before wearing gemstones.
