# EVENT DEFINITION STANDARD

To ensure deterministic scoring of prediction engines, the following event definitions are established.

## 1. CAREER & AUTHORITY
- **Career Success / Promotion**: Defined as a formal increase in responsibility, title upgrade, or significant public recognition of work within the predicted window.
- **Job Change**: A transition between employers or a major pivot in industry.
- **Business Launch**: The formal registration or first commercial transaction of a new venture.

## 2. FINANCE & WEALTH
- **Significant Gain**: A non-routine financial inflow (inheritance, bonus, investment return) exceeding 20% of annual income.
- **Financial Stress**: A period of unexpected expenditures or liquidity challenges reported as distinct from routine bills.

## 3. MARRIAGE & RELATIONSHIPS
- **Marriage**: Formal legal or traditional union ceremony.
- **Relationship Bond**: The start of a cohabiting partnership or formal engagement.
- **Relationship Stress**: A period of conflict leading to separation or significant emotional distance.

## 4. TRAVEL & ASSETS
- **Relocation**: A permanent or long-term change of residence (city or country).
- **Property Purchase**: Execution of a deed for real estate.
- **Foreign Travel**: Crossing international boundaries for 14+ days.

## 5. EDUCATION
- **Academic Success**: Passing a major certification, degree completion, or receiving an admission offer to a preferred institution.

## SCORING METRICS
- **EXACT MATCH**: Event occurs within ±15 days of predicted Peak.
- **BROAD MATCH**: Event occurs within the Preparation to End window.
- **PARTIAL MATCH**: Themes of the event were present but did not reach full manifestation (e.g., interviewed for promotion but didn't get it).
- **FALSE POSITIVE**: Strong prediction made, but user reports no relevant activity.
- **FALSE NEGATIVE**: Significant event occurred, but engine gave Weak/Mixed indication.
