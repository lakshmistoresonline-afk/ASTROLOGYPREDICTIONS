## 3. KEY DEFECT IDENTIFIED: "TRANSIT OVER-TRIGGER"
The root cause of the high False Positive Rate (FPR) was identified in the `TimingWindowEngine`. 
- **Mechanism**: The engine scans 180 days into the future from any simulation date. If *any* transit peak is found in that 6-month window, it applies the full `TRANSIT_TRIGGER` bonus (+15%) to the confluence score.
- **Effect**: Stable periods occurring within a strong Dasha are incorrectly elevated to "STRONG" confidence because the engine eventually finds a transit peak somewhere in the future.
