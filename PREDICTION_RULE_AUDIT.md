# PREDICTION RULE AUDIT

## 1. HIERARCHY OF RULES
Every domain now follows a strictly weighted hierarchy:
- **PRIMARY**: Dasha Activation (Antardasha Lord placement/lordship).
- **SECONDARY**: Natal D1 House placement, Lord strength, and Divisional Chart confirmation (D9/D10/D24/D4).
- **SUPPORTING**: Karaka strength, Yoga presence, Ashtakavarga SAV.

## 2. DOMAIN-SPECIFIC RULES

### Career & Authority
- **Primary**: Antardasha activation of 10/11/1.
- **Secondary**: 10th Lord in Kendra/Trikona. D10 confirmation (10th Lord well placed in Dashamsha).
- **Corroboration**: Needs 2+ signals for 'Strong', 3+ for 'Very Strong'.

### Finance & Wealth
- **Primary**: Antardasha of 2/11 lord or Jupiter.
- **Secondary**: 2nd Lord placement. D2 (Hora) benefic count.
- **Corroboration**: Heavily weighted on Jupiter's natal dignity.

### Marriage & Relationships
- **Primary**: Antardasha of 7th lord, Venus, or Jupiter.
- **Secondary**: 7th Lord in good house. D9 (Navamsa) l7 placement.
- **Corroboration**: Checks for malefics in 7th as negative modifiers (Conflict).

### Health & Vitality
- **Primary**: Lagna Lord strength (Exaltation/Debility).
- **Secondary**: Sun strength. 6th Lord placement.
- **Divisional**: D30 (Trishamsha) foundation check.

## 3. CONFLICT HANDLING
The engine no longer averages good and bad. 
- If 10th Lord is in Kendra but currently in a Dusthana Antardasha, the result is **MIXED** with an explicit "Conflict Detected" warning.

## 4. CONCLUSION
The rules are now consistent with classical Parashari principles and avoid the "Single Factor Bias".
